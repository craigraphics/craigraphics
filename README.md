# Hey there, I'm William Craig!

I'm a senior software engineer with 15+ years building for the web. These days, most of my work is focused on AI platforms, agents, and practical tools that make everyday work easier.

My background is in visual design and frontend development, and that still shapes how I build. I care about how a system works, how people use it, and the small details that make it feel finished.

## What I'm working on

At Disney, I work on AI platform infrastructure, orchestrating multiple LLMs and building observability for a self-service assistant. That work brings together architecture, reliability, and the experience of the person using the product.

I'm also building tools that help AI agents work with better context. One is a knowledge base that connects the projects I work on and uses vector search to bring relevant information into the development process.

Another is a conversational learning proof of concept. It asks questions to understand what someone already knows about AI, finds gaps, and helps them work out what to learn next. The idea came from a problem I've had myself: it's hard to ask the right questions when you don't yet understand a subject.

## How I work

I use AI throughout development, while staying involved in architecture, usability, and verification. I spend more of my time checking that the idea is being implemented correctly, looking for gaps, and making sure a fix addresses the actual problem.
